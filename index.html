System design

Online Food Ordering System

A group requirements analysis and modeling project that plans how customers browse restaurants, order food and pay online, and how restaurant staff and administrators run the service.

  • A UML use case diagram with a subject boundary, three primary actors (customer, restaurant staff and administrator) on the left, and one secondary actor (the payment gateway) on the right
  • Relationships: place order includes make payment, apply voucher code extends place order, and pay cash on delivery and pay online are specialized types of make payment
  • 8 functional requirements, such as accounts, cart, ordering, payment choice, order tracking and status updates
  • 5 non-functional requirements covering security, performance and operational needs, such as password and payment encryption, fast page loads, support for many users and use on any platform
Web design

Sip Club website

A responsive website concept for a bubble tea shop, made as a student UI design exercise.

  • Four sections: a home page, a menu of four favorite drinks with prices, a “why us” highlights row, and shop hours with an order call-to-action
  • A bold, playful style with thick outlines, offset shadows, the Fredoka font and a yellow, pink and sky blue palette
  • Built with HTML and CSS only, using Grid, Flexbox and light and dark modes
  • Responsive: the menu goes from four columns to two to one on smaller screens
